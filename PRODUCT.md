## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

React / Next.js with Tailwind CSS (Selected for future implementation; currently in planning phase with no code written)

## Users

Individuals seeking to record, reflect upon, and understand dreams across both traditional spiritual and scientific frameworks. Communicates naturally in English, Tamil, or Tanglish.

## Product Purpose

DreamLens 2.0 is a multilingual AI-powered dream interpretation and journaling platform. It enables users to explore and reflect on their dreams through two independent, rigorous perspectives: traditional Hindu scriptural wisdom and research-grounded psychological analysis, both anchored in source-based retrieval with citations.

## Positioning

Unlike superficial AI chatbots or generic dream dictionaries that offer shallow or hallucinated symbolism, DreamLens 2.0 delivers two sovereign, cited viewpoints—Hindu scriptural tradition and scientific psychology—with native multilingual fluency (English, Tamil, Tanglish).

## Operating Context

Personal, reflective journaling environments across mobile and web screens (often morning dream capture or quiet nighttime reflection). Requires high trust, privacy, calm focus, and seamless script switching between Tamil and Latin characters.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dual independent interpretation lenses: Hindu scriptural tradition and research-backed psychology.
- Source-grounded retrieval with explicit citations for every perspective.
- Multilingual conversational and journaling capabilities in English, Tamil, and Tanglish.
- Constraint: Currently in planning phase; no frontend or backend implementation until explicitly authorized.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: DreamLens 2.0
- Tone & Atmosphere: Mysterious, thoughtful, trustworthy, and professionally designed.
- Balance: Reverent toward sacred traditional literature, rigorous and objective toward psychological research.

## Evidence on Hand

- Greenfield repository (`DreamLens-2.0`); no incumbent legacy codebase or assets.
- Initial product truth established via user-confirmed planning specification.

## Product Principles

- **Sovereign Perspectives:** Never conflate scriptural and psychological lenses into a generic synthesis; present each with its authentic logic, depth, and terminology.
- **Source-Grounded Integrity:** Every interpretation must link to verifiable scriptural or psychological research citations.
- **Multilingual Dignity:** Treat Tamil and Tanglish as first-class linguistic experiences with thoughtful typographic and contextual support, not second-class machine translations.
- **Contemplative Trust:** Cultivate an atmosphere that feels deeply thoughtful and mysterious without resorting to gimmicks or superstition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Robust support for Tamil Unicode typography alongside Latin script.
- Adaptive layouts tailored for both handheld mobile journaling and expansive desktop exploration.
- High visual legibility suitable for low-light morning/evening reflective reading.
