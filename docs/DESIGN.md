# DreamLens 2.0 — Visual Design System & Creative Direction

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 2 -->

## 1. Design Vision & Philosophy: "Psychological Surrealism + Sacred Narrative Design"

DreamLens 2.0 firmly rejects generic AI aesthetics: overused neon gradients, cookie-cutter cards, unmotivated glassmorphism, and corporate dashboard templates. Instead, it embodies **Psychological Surrealism + Sacred Narrative Design**:
- **Controlled Asymmetry:** Editorial layouts with intentional rhythm, expansive negative space, and typographic gravitas.
- **Atmospheric Storytelling:** Every visual element—from linework to lighting—evokes deep emotion rather than empty SaaS polish.
- **Two Sovereign Visual Realms:** One unified product housing two philosophically and aesthetically distinct worlds:
  1. **The Psychology Module:** Cerebral, introspective, surreal, monochromatic depth, sharp editorial typography.
  2. **The Hindu Mythology Module:** Ancient, literary, illuminated manuscript aesthetic, warm terracotta and antique copper, sacred cosmic geometry.

## 2. Three-Layer Color Architecture

### Layer 1: Global Identity (Shared Frame & Navigation)
- **Deep Petrol / Void Black:** `#070a0f` (Primary canvas)
- **Smoky Slate:** `#121824` (Subtle container tone)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Hairline architectural boundaries)
- **Soft Chalk / Ivory:** `#f0f2f5` (High-contrast typography)
- **Muted Steel:** `#8492a6` (Secondary UI labels and metadata)
- **Astral Cyan Accent:** `#38bdf8` (Micro-indicators, zero-auth security tags)

### Layer 2: Psychology Module ("The Subconscious Mind")
*Mood: Introspective, subconscious, cerebral, enigmatic, soft-edged shadows.*
- **Deep Ink Navy:** `#060810` (Background base)
- **Abyssal Slate:** `#0d121d` (Elevated panel surface)
- **Faded Violet / Muted Plum:** `#1a162b` (Affective highlighting and deep reflection)
- **Stone Gray:** `#4e5868` (Tertiary lines and subtle grounding)
- **Sage Whispers:** `#7e9a8e` (Insight highlights and empirical verification badges)
- **Spectral Silver:** `#e2e8f0` (Headlines and primary reading text)
- **Nocturnal Amber:** `#c27803` (Uncertainty indicators and symbol tension points)

### Layer 3: Hindu Mythology Module ("The Sacred Cosmic Vision")
*Mood: Ancient, sacred, literary, timeless, reverent, illuminated manuscript.*

- **Sanctum Charcoal:** `#18110b` (Parchment panel base)
- **Aged Manuscript Parchment:** `#fbf5eb` (Main reading text with soothing warmth)

## 3. Dual Typography System

### Global / Shared UI
- **Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, `sans-serif`
- **Use:** Navigation, form inputs, language selectors, button labels, technical metadata.

### Psychology World
- **Display & Section Headers:** `Cormorant Garamond` / `Cinzel` (Sharp, elegant, moody, psychoanalytic rigor).
- **Body & Explanations:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` with generous line-height (`leading-relaxed`) and deliberate tracking.
- **Scholarly & Empirical Citations:** `JetBrains Mono` for exact page, chapter, and neurocognitive citations.

### Hindu Mythology World
- **Display & Main Headers:** `Mordial` (primary bespoke archaic serif), fallback to `Rozha One` / `Cinzel Decorative` (Statuesque, Indian architectural presence).
- **Scriptural Verses & Translations:** `Lora` (Warm humanist literary serif, reminiscent of sacred book print).
- **Tamil & Sanskrit Sacred Typography:** `Noto Serif Tamil` and Sanskrit glyphs with full diacritical dignity.

## 4. Visual Storytelling & Imagery Rules

- **Zero Generic Stock Photos:** No random purple neural meshes or 3D glowing robot heads.
- **Psychology Storytelling:** Surrealist fine-art compositions—shadowed doorways, layered reflections, fractured mirrors, subconscious horizons, fine-etching vignettes.
- **Mythology Storytelling:** Sacred cosmic geometry (*Yantras*), celestial sun & moon chariots, temple threshold arches, lotus petal geometry, and illuminated manuscript borders.
- **Prahara Cosmic Indicator:** Visual temporal clock reflecting the 4 watches of the night from Agni Purana Chapter 229.

---

## 5. Layout & Interaction Architecture

- **Controlled Asymmetry:** Avoid predictable identical boxes. Combine wide narrative blocks with narrow scholarly rails.
- **Intimate Dream Input Sanctuary:** A distraction-free, quiet space that honors the dreamer's subconscious without UI clutter.
- **Editorial Reading Experience:** Treat dream analysis as a bespoke literary publication rather than a chatbot response.
- **Sovereign World Transitions:** Switching between Psychology and Mythology cross-fades background ambiance, typography, and ornamentation while strictly preserving user inputs.
