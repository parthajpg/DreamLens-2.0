# DreamLens 2.0 — Visual Design System & Creative Direction

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## 1. Design Vision & Guiding Philosophy
DreamLens 2.0 rejects generic modern SaaS aesthetics (uniform gradients, generic cards, and rounded chat bubbles). Instead, it presents an editorial, cinematic dual-world journey where entering either **Psychology** or **Hindu Mythology** transforms the screen into a distinct visual realm.

The two worlds are **not** a simple light/dark toggle. They feature independent color palettes, typography scales, atmospheric depths, ornamentation, and transition physics.

---

## 2. World I: The Psychology World ("The Subconscious Mind")

### 2.1 Emotional & Atmospheric Experience
- **Core Mood:** Introspection, psychological depth, atmospheric shadows, controlled tension, unsolved questions, stillness.
- **Visual Metaphor:** The layered architecture of the psyche; subterranean depths, dim archival libraries, nocturnal clarity.

### 2.2 Color System
- **Void Obsidian (Background):** `#0a0b0e` (rich ink-black with cold undertones)
- **Subconscious Charcoal (Elevations/Surfaces):** `#14171f`
- **Tonal Border / Shadow:** `#242938`
- **Neuro Slate (Muted Text / Secondary):** `#8b9bb4`
- **Luminous Silver (Primary Headers / Text):** `#f0f3f8`
- **Insight Accent (Subtle Electric Indigo):** `#5865f2` (focused micro-accents only, never dominant)
- **Tension Ochre (Symbol highlights):** `#d97706`

### 2.3 Typography System
- **Display / Headers:** Editorial Serif (e.g., *Cinzel*, *Playfair Display*, or *Cormorant Garamond*) — conveying classical psychoanalysis and rigorous scholarly depth.
- **Body & Citations:** Clean, highly legible Grotesk Mono/Sans (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *JetBrains Mono* for citations).
- **Stylistic Characteristics:** Generous letter-spacing on subheadings (`tracking-widest`), high contrast headings against obsidian backgrounds.

### 2.4 Interaction & Motion Language
- **Transitions:** Slow, contemplative fade-ins with subtle vertical drift.
- **Shadows:** Deep, diffuse multi-layered drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`).
- **Borders:** Ultra-fine hairline borders (`border-white/10`).

---

## 3. World II: The Hindu Mythology World ("The Beyond & Sacred Cosmic Dream")

### 3.1 Emotional & Atmospheric Experience
- **Core Mood:** Wonder, illumination, sacred cosmic stillness, ancient scriptural wisdom, celestial depth.
- **Visual Metaphor:** Temple sanctums at dawn, sacred fire light, celestial mandalas, ancient palm-leaf manuscripts (*Talapatra*), brass oil lamps (*Deepam*).

### 3.2 Color System
- **Sacred Midnight / Deep Temple Umber (Background):** `#0d0907` (warm deep mahogany black)
- **Sanctum Bronze (Surfaces / Panels):** `#18120e`
- **Ancient Gold (Primary Accent & Headings):** `#e5b869`
- **Saffron Glow (Secondary Highlights):** `#f59e0b` / `#d97706`
- **Vedic Parchment (Body Text):** `#f7eee1` (soft warm ivory, soothing to the eyes)
- **Sacred Sandalwood / Muted Terracotta (Borders & Dividers):** `#3d2b22`
- **Auspicious Vermilion (Micro-accents):** `#b91c1c`

### 3.3 Typography System
- **Display / Headers:** Regal, classical serif with Indian sculptural undertones (e.g., *Rozha One*, *Cinzel Decorative*, or *Yatra One*).
- **Tamil Typography:** Highly crafted Tamil serif/sans (e.g., *Noto Serif Tamil*, *Mukta Malar*, or *Kavivanar*).
- **Body:** Warm, humanist serif/sans (*Lora* or *Eczar*) for comfortable reading of long scriptural passages.

### 3.4 Interaction & Motion Language
- **Transitions:** Radiant glow reveals, subtle shimmering golden borders, gentle breathing luminosity.
- **Ornamentation:** Refined, authentic sacred geometric motifs (fine jali patterns, lotus-inspired hairline dividers) used with restraint and reverence. Never cartoonish or kitsch.

---

## 4. Shared Brand Foundation & Navigation
- **Top Brand Anchor:** DreamLens 2.0 brandmark with dual-colored lens iris.
- **Perspective Switcher:** An elegant dual-state toggle: "Mind (Psychology)" ⟷ "Beyond (Mythology)" that triggers an atmospheric canvas transition.
- **Language Selector:** Seamless pill dropdown: English | தமிழ் (Tamil) | Tanglish.
- **Private Journal Drawer:** Sliding minimalist drawer with clear indicators that all data is strictly stored in device memory.
